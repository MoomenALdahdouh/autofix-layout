Common answers

I installed it. Nothing happens.

Automatic correction needs at least two keyboards enabled and waits for Space, Enter, or Tab. It will not change a word it is unsure about.

How do I use only the converter?

Turn Automatic correction off. Leave Manual converter on.

How do I activate Pro?

Complete checkout when the store link is live, then paste the Lemon Squeezy license key in the popup Settings.
