You start typing in Arabic.

Then you notice your keyboard was still set to English. Now you have to delete everything and type it again. Layfix removes that interruption.

How it works

1. Keep typing

Write normally. Layfix stays quiet until a word is finished.

2. It notices the layout

Layfix recognizes when the keys match another keyboard you use.

3. Your text is restored

The letters you meant appear. Real words stay as typed.

Your language can change from word to word

Layfix does not force a sentence into one language.

Questions

What is Layfix?

A browser extension that restores text typed on the wrong keyboard layout.

Does Layfix translate text?

No. It remaps the same physical keys to the keyboard you meant.

Does it correct spelling?

No. Real words stay as you typed them.

What languages are supported?

The keyboards you enable in the popup. Chinese, Japanese, and Korean IMEs are not layout remaps.

Can I disable automatic correction?

Yes. The manual converter can stay on by itself.

Can I manually convert text?

Yes. Type or paste in the popup. Conversion is instant and local.

Does Layfix store my text?

Manual converter text is not stored. A short local history of automatic corrections can stay on your device. That list is not uploaded.

Does Layfix send text to a server?

Only for automatic correction when a local decision is not enough. See the Privacy Policy.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

You move to Free: a daily active-use allowance for automatic correction. The manual converter stays available.

How does Free usage work?

Only active typing in supported fields counts. Idle time does not. The allowance refills over time, up to two hours.

What is Pro?

Unlimited automatic correction for $29 / year.
